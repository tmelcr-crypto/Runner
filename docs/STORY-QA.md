# The whole story - questions and answers

The owner asked (October 2026) for the whole story with logical consequences: a main story plus storylines that can be played on their
own, not connected to each other, that tie together around the end of the main story - about 70 quests in all - and for at least 100
questions before building it. Each answer is written here as it comes; the story is built from this page.

## Answers

1. Split: a main story of 30 quests + 5 side storylines of about 8 quests each.
2. Quests 01-06 (the Varga story) become one of the side storylines (the main story starts elsewhere).
3. Each side storyline brings something to the finale (an ally, a vehicle, a place or a secret the finale uses; the people you helped show up at the end).
4. No side storyline is required: the finale opens when the main story reaches it; each side storyline finished makes it easier or different.
5. The player: a newcomer with nothing - arrives broke, no past anyone knows, rises by doing jobs for people.
6. The player speaks like everyone else (full lines in cutscenes and live lines; a portrait and a mumble of their own).
7. The main story's enemy: a developer who owns the city - buying every block (Skyline One, the Colony, the casino strip) with dirty money, police and politicians in his pocket.
8. The main story: from nobody to running the city.
9. The player's name: Danny Cruz (Marco still calls him "boatman").
10. One fixed hero (one portrait, one voice; clothes still change in the shops).
11. The developer: Victor Crane - Crane Development, his name on the cranes.
12. Crane is a name you hear long before you meet him; you meet him in person only late in the story.
13. Set in 1997, the year before Skyline One opens (pagers, car phones, VHS; the 90s radio).
14. Danny arrives on the airport's last flight of the night - a borrowed suit and someone else's suitcase.
15. He came to find his missing older brother, who came here a year ago and stopped writing; the trail leads, slowly, to Crane.
16. The city is called Miravista.
17. The brother: Mateo Cruz is alive and works for Crane as his driver and fixer; he is hiding something and is trying to get out.
18. The suitcase holds nothing important - someone's holiday clothes, a running joke through the story.
19. Danny finds Mateo only at the very end.
20. Danny's first friend: an airport taxi driver who picks him up on the first night, talks too much, knows everyone and gives him the first jobs.
21. The taxi driver: Papa Joe, 70 - the oldest cabbie at the airport; he has seen everything, tells stories and knows where the bodies are buried.
22. Mateo hides proof of Crane's crimes (a ledger or a tape that could bring Crane down); he stopped writing to keep Danny out of danger.
23. The main story is 5 chapters of 6 quests.
24. The Varga storyline (Marco and Lucia) opens after a set main quest.
25. The Varga storyline opens with the last quest of chapter 1 (main quest 6: Papa Joe drops Danny at the quay with a job for Marco); from then on it can be played any time.
26. The 5 chapters: 1 Arrival (Papa Joe's jobs, the first money); 2 The street (gangs, a name in the city); 3 The trail (Mateo's clues, Crane's men); 4 The war (taking Crane's businesses); 5 The fall (Crane, Mateo, the finale).
27. The whole city is open in STORY mode from the start, as in FREE ROAM; the quests just happen in different districts.
28. Papa Joe's look: white hair and a white moustache, a flat cap, a brown cardigan over a shirt, reading glasses on a cord, a toothpick.
29. The Vargas are Crane's front men: Nico builds Skyline One on Crane's money; bringing Nico down in Quest 06 is a first blow to Crane, though Danny does not know it yet.
30. The 4 new side storylines: the Casino (a casino dealer, an inside man), the Colony (the hotel's owner, whom Crane wants out), the Prison (a lifer on Iron Key who knows Crane's past) and the Space center (a scientist).
31. The space center's entry (STORY open item 1): both, by quest - a pass from a quest first; later a disguise (a lab coat or a flight suit from the clothes) also gets Danny past the guards.
32. The Varga line stays at 6 quests: 30 main + 6 Varga + 4 x 8 new = 68 quests.
33. The Casino storyline is led by Del Ramos, the nervous croupier from Quest 03 who kept the Vargas' books; now he wants out from under Crane's casino bosses and needs Danny.
34. Its goal: eight quests that build up to one big heist of the Royal Flamingo's vault, where Crane washes his dirty money.
35. It brings a getaway to the finale (the casino's armoured car or helicopter for the escape).
36. It opens at the start of chapter 2, when Danny has a name on the street: a marker D on the casino strip at night.
37. The Colony Hotel is owned by Esther Bloom, 70s - a sharp old widow who has run it since the 50s; pearls, sunglasses, a cigarette holder; she refuses every offer from Crane. She leads the Colony storyline.
38. The Colony storyline: save the hotel from Crane, who wants it torn down for a tower - his thugs, a fire, bought inspectors, a forged deed; Danny beats each move.
39. It brings a base and its people to the finale: the hotel becomes Danny's headquarters for the war; its staff and guests come out to help at the end.
40. Danny's first bed in STORY: a Colony room - Papa Joe takes him there on the first night and Esther gives him a room on credit (the free safehouse of FREE ROAM).
41. The Prison storyline is led by Sal Moretti, 60s, a lifer on Iron Key - Crane's first partner in the 70s, framed by Crane for a murder Crane did; 20 years inside, calm, patient, plays chess.
42. Its goal: break him out - eight quests build a slow escape plan (guards, a boat, papers, a riot as cover); the last quest is the breakout.
43. It brings Crane's past and a way in to the finale: the truth about Crane's first murder (it backs up Mateo's proof) and a secret way into Crane's place that Sal knows.
44. It opens at the start of chapter 3, with the trail: a letter from Sal reaches Danny - someone on the inside knows Crane's past.
45. The Space center storyline is led by Dr. Iris Kahn, 40s - a rocket engineer, brilliant and stubborn, a lab coat and big glasses; her last launch is all that keeps the center open.
46. Its story: save the last launch - Crane wants Gravel Flats for a resort; if the rocket fails the center closes. Danny stops his sabotage, quest by quest, up to the launch.
47. It brings an eye in the sky to the finale: the launched satellite (or the center's radar) shows every one of Crane's men on the map.
48. It opens at the start of chapter 4, with the war on Crane (his resort plan for Gravel Flats is one of his businesses).
49. Crane has four lieutenants: the muscle (a gang boss who does the dirty work), the cop (the police chief), the politician (a city councilman) and the money (his lawyer, who moves the money); Danny takes them down one by one.
50. Victor Crane's look: 50s, silver hair swept back, a deep tan, a white linen suit, a gold watch - the smiling face on the billboards.
51. Crane's own place, the scene of the finale: the Pearl Key estate - his walled mansion on Pearl Key with gates, guards, a dock and a helipad.
52. The city loves Crane: a builder running for mayor, his posters everywhere, the TV on his side; only the street knows the truth.
53. The lieutenants' names: Bruno "Bull" Kessler (the muscle, a Dockside gang boss), Chief Ray Holloway (the police), Councilman Dean Pratt (the politician) and Leonard Sachs (the lawyer).
54. Their fall: Kessler at the end of chapter 2, Sachs at the end of chapter 3, Holloway and Pratt in chapter 4, Crane in chapter 5.
55. Mateo's trail: a clue each chapter (an address, his car at the casino, a photo with Crane, his voice on a tape) - and someone unseen helps Danny a few times; at the end it was Mateo.
56. Mateo turns on Crane: face to face at the end he hands Danny the proof and fights beside him against Crane; both brothers live.
