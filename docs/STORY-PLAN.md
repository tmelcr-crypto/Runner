# Miravista, 1997 - the story plan

The whole STORY mode in one plan, made from the owner's 108 answers (`docs/STORY-QA.md`, October 2026), for the owner to approve
before any quest is built. Each quest gets its own Word page when it is built (the quest pages of `docs/STORY.md`), goes into
MISSIONS TEST, and STORY is put together in one piece once every quest is approved. Everything here can still change.

## At a glance

- **68 quests**: the main story of 30 (5 chapters of 6) and five side storylines - the Varga line (the 6 quests already built) and
  four new ones of 8: the Casino, the Prison, the Colony and the Space center.
- **The hero**: Danny Cruz, 30s, a buzz cut and a scar, who lands on the last flight of the night with a borrowed suit and someone
  else's suitcase, looking for his missing older brother Mateo.
- **The enemy**: Victor Crane, the builder whose name is on every crane in Miravista, running for mayor. Danny hears the name on the
  first night and meets the man only in chapter 5.
- **The side storylines** can be played in any order, are not connected to each other, and none is required. Each one finished
  brings a gift to the finale; each one missing makes the finale harder.
- **Four big choices** in the main story, made by what Danny does, change later quests and a few scenes of the one ending.
- **After the finale** the game goes on: Danny is the quiet boss of Miravista with a daily income; unfinished side lines can still be played.

## The truth behind the story

What really happened, told to the player bit by bit:

- **1975**: Vic Crane and his partner Sal Moretti built the Pearl Key estate on land its owner would not sell. The owner was found
  dead; Crane killed him and framed Sal, who has been on Iron Key ever since. Crane dug the estate's cellar with Sal - and forgot
  the old smugglers' tunnel Sal dug from the boathouse.
- **1996**: Mateo Cruz came to Miravista, worked as a mechanic at Manny's Auto, and took a job as Crane's driver. He drove Crane and
  his lawyer Sachs for months, copied Sachs' ledger of who Crane pays, and taped Crane ordering a killing in the back of the car.
  Afraid for his brother, he stopped writing home. He has been trying to get out ever since, with the proof hidden.
- **1997**: Crane runs for mayor; his front men the Vargas build Skyline One; Bull Kessler does his dirty work on the street, Chief
  Ray Holloway keeps the police off him, Councilman Dean Pratt sells him the city's land and Leonard Sachs moves the money.
  Danny lands, and Mateo, who sees his brother walking into his world, starts helping him in secret.

## The main story

Each chapter opens with a title card (its name, no date). Quests start at the giver's marker (the letter in a circle) or by a call:
in chapter 1 the **pager** buzzes and Danny calls back from any phone box; from chapter 2 he carries a **brick mobile phone**. A
quest that needs an hour moves the clock in its opening cutscene. Pay grows from about €300 to €20,000 a quest.

## Running jokes and nods

- **The suitcase**: someone else's holiday clothes (flower shirts, a snorkel, far too much sunscreen). Papa Joe keeps trying to give
  it back; Danny wears its shirt once when his own is ruined (a free flower shirt in the wardrobe); its owner turns up after the credits.
- **Papa Joe's cab** is wrecked again and again (M05, M19, ...), each time with a story about the one before.
- **Nods**: once a side line is done, a line or two in the next main quests mentions it - Joe heard about the vault on his radio,
  Nina covers Sal's escape on the news, Rosa saw the rocket go up.

## What has to be built for the story

Asked about one by one (FREE ROAM, STORY or both) when each is built:

1. **STORY mode itself**: chapters and their title cards; the givers' markers by letter; unlocking quests, lines, safehouses and
   gifts; saving between quests; story flags (the four choices, the lines done).
2. **The pager and the phone boxes** (chapter 1), then the brick phone.
3. **Choices by action** in the mission engine (shoot or walk away; drive to A or to B).
4. **Danny**: a row in the character table; his portrait drawn from his face plus the top and hat he wears.
5. **New characters**: rows and portraits for Papa Joe, Esther, Crane, Mateo, Kessler, Rosa, Nina, Sachs, Holloway, Pratt, Sal and
   Iris, then the minor people.
6. **Quest kinds**: stealth (seen or not), tailing, defending a place in waves, fragile and timed deliveries.
7. **Disguises**: guards that check the clothes (the space center's pass, lab coat and flight suit; the casino guard's uniform;
   Dunn's spare uniform); new clothes for them, Danny's borrowed suit and the suitcase's flower shirt.
8. **Places**: Kessler's gym, Rosa's market (stalls in Mercado), Manny's Auto, the pawnshop, the bookie, the betting shop, the
   courthouse, the FBI office, the lunapark lockers, the war room, the Pearl Key estate's house, boathouse, tunnel and helipad (inside,
   top-down) and Crane's jet at Skyport.
9. **Vehicles**: the casino's armoured car, the news van, the produce truck, the fuel truck and the laundry truck (where the
   vehicle table has none that fits).
10. **The daily income** after the finale, in the economy table.
