THE GAME

  • FREE ROAM: no missions, the city is yours. Steal cars, cause trouble, collect cash and stay alive. STORY mode is coming later.
  • You start at the door of your room at the Colony hotel; your car waits in its reserved space in the guests' lot.
  • Listen to the city: traffic, horns (stand in front of a stopped car and its driver honks), brakes, people talking, birds by day, crickets at night, the surf by the sea, far-off sirens and dogs. In a car it is muffled.
  • POINTS

CARS

  • Walk up to one and press E: an empty car takes a second, pulling the driver out takes three (press again to give up).
  • Fast cars slide, heavy ones push through. Wet roads are slippery.
  • A damaged car shows it: white wisps, then thick grey smoke, then black smoke and flames from the bonnet. On fire all over, bail out - it blows.
  • DRIVE-BY: with a pistol, revolver, SMG or machine gun in hand, FIRE in a car shoots at the nearest target ahead. Otherwise you get in with grenades in hand, to drop out of the window.
  • FIRE ENGINE: FIRE sprays the water cannon - it knocks people down and puts out burning cars.
  • APC: hold FIRE - the turret machine gun turns to the nearest target ahead; a belt of 100, then a reload (shown under the speed). TANK: FIRE launches rockets.
  • Most cars have the radio on: R or RADIO tunes to the next station.
  • Somewhere far from where you start, a tank waits in a parking lot.

JOBS

  • Drive the right vehicle into the marker at its dispatch point and stop: the START card shows. TAXI at a taxi rank (yellow cab sign on the maps), PARAMEDIC in an ambulance at a hospital, FIREFIGHTER in a fire engine at a fire station (flame on the maps), VIGILANTE in a police car at a police station.
  • Level 1 asks for one fare, patient, fire or criminal car, each level for one more. Each adds time to the clock and pays; a level done pays a bonus. The job ends when the time runs out, the vehicle is wrecked or you are out of it for 10 s - police stars do not end it.
  • TAXI: the rank sends you to a fare; stop by them, then at the destination. Fast trips tip more, crashes cost the tip. PARAMEDIC: up to 3 patients aboard, then any hospital; crashes cost time. FIREFIGHTER: spray burning cars with the water cannon until they are out. VIGILANTE: wreck the criminals' car or take them down when they run; from level 5 they shoot back.
  • Level 8 once: NITRO in taxis (Shift or N, or the NITRO button), +50 max health, fireproof, +50 max body armor.
  • Tap or click the job's panel at the top to STOP the job. A police car in a police station's yard is yours for the vigilante job - no crime.

RACES

  • STREET RACES: now and then a phone booth near you rings (a blinking phone on the maps). On foot at it, tap ANSWER or press E. A double ring is one of five fixed races (best times kept), a fast trill a random one.
  • ACCEPT pays the fee (not given back). You have 60 s to stop at the start in any car but a public service vehicle - not while wanted. It counts down 3 s; pass every checkpoint in order. Prizes for 1st, 2nd and 3rd.
  • From the countdown to the results you cannot get out or use weapons.
  • SPEEDWAY on the Palm Heights beach: at the race booth in the paddock tap RACE - you are put in a NASCAR special on the grid, 5 laps against 7 others. A NASCAR special also stands in the paddock to take.
  • DRAG STRIPS: the Palm strip beside the speedway (201 m) and the long Sandbar strip on the east beach (600 m). Stop by a strip's booth in a car: RACE a rival, or a free TEST RUN for your top speed, 0-100 and split times. Three ambers, then green - go. When it is over, the green EXIT gate past the finish lets you out toward the city (only from inside).
  • A faded LIME LINE on the streets shows the way during a street race, a job, or with the car wanted at the docks.
  • The results show for 10 s: close them with CLOSE, E or ENTER.
  • A GLOW on the screen edge points the way while it is off screen: lime to your next target, yellow or cyan (as on the maps) to a ringing phone.

PICKUPS

  • CASH stacks lie on sidewalks around town, not on the maps; the dead drop cash too, and every car has some inside.
  • ARMED PEOPLE some passers-by carry a pistol or a melee weapon: hurt them, or someone next to them, and they fight back. They drop it when killed.
  • WEAPONS you have to find: only your fists to start with. Each weapon and its ammo float in a bubble of the weapon's own colour, hidden in alleys, yards, parks, parking lots and on the beaches. Melee weapons are the most common and always lie in the same places.
  • Hidden the same way, each in its own bubble: HEALTH (white, +50 when you are hurt), BODY ARMOR (blue, +50) and the HEAT REDUCER (flashing red and blue: one star less while you are wanted). None of it shows on the maps.
  • More HEAT REDUCERS: from 3 stars one lies in the middle of a street ahead of you, and ten wait in back alleys - always there, taken only while you are wanted, back two minutes after.
  • BODY ARMOR is the blue bar under your health: it takes the damage first.
  • All of it is picked up ON FOOT only - except heat reducers: drive through them.

CLOTHES

  • Ten clothes shops (a coat hanger on the maps), each with its own range: hats, glasses, tops, bottoms, shoes.
  • Tap a piece and your figure on the turntable tries it on; tap it again to buy it. WARDROBE: what you own, free to put on at any clothes shop.
  • New clothes while no cop saw you go in: the police lose you (up to 3 stars; above that it drops to 3).

PAY 'N' SPRAY

  • Four paint shops (a spray can on the maps). Drive up in a car and the door rolls up; drive in and stop - the door comes down and the roof turns see-through.
  • Tap a colour - its own paints or any colour - and the car shows it. SPRAY: €100, repaired and repainted. LEAVE: nothing paid.
  • No cop saw you go in: the police lose you, at any level. Taxis, buses, ambulances, fire and trash trucks are repaired only; police and army vehicles are turned away.

SAFEHOUSES

  • Ten places to own: the Colony room is yours; the others carry a FOR SALE sign - walk up and BUY (€3,000 to €150,000). A house on the maps: green yours, white with a € for sale.
  • Towers: GO IN and you see the building from the side - lobby, stairs, elevator, neighbours, your door. Houses: the doors open for you and the roof turns see-through.
  • SLEEP: saves in a slot, full health and armour, wake at 8:00 or 20:00. WARDROBE: all your clothes. STASH: put weapons away - kept even when wasted or busted.
  • Leave a car in your garage or your reserved space and it stays there, saved. Going in unseen loses the police.

CAR SHOWROOM

  • SUNSTRIP MOTORS on the beach avenue (a pink car on the maps): every car kind on a turntable, and three rare ones sold only there - the VELOCE, the SUNDANCER and the BRUISER.
  • Walk up to a car and BUY: pick its colour and a free garage bay or reserved space of a safehouse you own - it waits for you there.
  • TUNING behind it: drive in and stop. Paint, roof, stripes, rims, underglow, a spoiler and a scoop, and three levels of engine, brakes and armour - tap to try them on the car, BUY pays for the changes. Not for police cars, nor while you are wanted. The tuning stays with the car.

JUNKYARD

  • DOCKSIDE SALVAGE at the port (a magnet over a cube on the maps): drive onto the yellow spot inside the gate, stop and get out - the crane lifts the car into the crusher and you are paid for the cube, less for damage.
  • Mind the guard dog on foot. Crushing a police car with a cop watching is a crime.

STATS AND 100%

  • STATS in the pause menu: your kills, cars stolen, distance, top speed and more, your best race times and job levels, and how much of the city you have done.
  • 100% counts each thing once: safehouses bought, rampages passed, hidden weapons and items found (walk up to the bubble), races won, jobs to level 10, 10 cars delivered at the docks, every Pay 'n' Spray used.
  • At 100%: health and armour up to 200, and a one-of-a-kind gold AUREA waits in your best garage - lose it and a new one is back there 5 minutes later.

STORES

  • Six stores, each with a € in its own colour on the maps, its name over the door and a ring of light at it. Each sells its own weapons with their ammo, and health or body armor - not everything in one place.
  • The BLACK MARKET at the docks sells the AA LAUNCHER (€25,000) and its rockets (€5,000): keep a helicopter, tank or APC inside the sight for 2 s until it locks, then let go - one rocket destroys it. You carry three at most; one more launcher lies hidden in the city.
  • Walk up to the door: tap SHOP or press E. The game waits while you shop. Your score is your cash.

RAMPAGES

  • Fifty red skulls are hidden around the city (twenty in story mode). Once you have been near one it shows on the maps (green once passed).
  • On foot at a skull, tap RAMPAGE or press E to see the goal, then START: kill so many people, or wreck so many vehicles, with the weapon you are given before the clock runs out.
  • The weapon stays in your hand with endless ammo (reloads still take time), you stay on foot, and the police look the other way. You cannot start one while you are wanted.
  • The first pass pays cash and you keep the weapon. You can play a passed rampage again, without the reward.

WASTED OR BUSTED

  • WASTED you wake up at the nearest hospital: the bill is 10% of your cash.
  • BUSTED you walk out of the prison gate on IRON KEY: bail is 10%, and they keep every weapon you found. The ferry waits at the pier.

IRON KEY

  • The prison island in the bay west of Heron Key (orange bars on the maps). Its free FERRY (on foot only) shuttles to the pier off the Palm Heights promenade (a cyan boat): walk aboard over the ramp, walk about the deck, walk off at the far end. SKIP THE CROSSING or E goes straight across. It won't sail while you are wanted.
  • Inside the walls is restricted: leave before the countdown ends, or the alarm gives 3 stars and the tower guards open fire.

THE CASINO STRIP

  • On the Sunstrip beach avenue: the ROYAL FLAMINGO casino (a pink chip on the maps), THE PULSE nightclub beside it (a heartbeat) and a row of bars across the avenue.
  • The bouncers want you dressed smart - one smart piece is enough (a suit, the silk shirt, a fedora, aviators, loafers, pressed trousers) - and never let you in while you are wanted.
  • In the casino: tap SLOTS at a machine (a pull is €10, three flamingos win the jackpot) or DICE at a table (7 or 11 wins, 2, 3 or 12 loses, any other number is the point - roll it again before a 7).
  • Trouble inside brings the armed guards and the police. With a gun at the cashier's window you can ROB the cage: stay there while the bag is filled.
  • THE PULSE opens at 22:00: walk past the queue, dance floor, DJ, music.

SKYLINE ONE

  • The construction site downtown in Palm Heights (a crane on the maps). Drive or walk up the half-built tower's ramps, deck by deck; the launch ramp on the top deck sends you over the avenue - onto the street, or onto the roofs across it if you are fast. A hard landing damages the car.
  • Up on a roof you can drive about or get out; off the edge you drop, and a long drop hurts.
  • By day the gate is open: the yard's ramps and mounds throw a fast car, and the mixer, the digger and the dump truck are yours to take. A weapon waits on the top deck, health or armour three decks up.
  • At night the gate is locked; get in on foot through the broken panel on the north street - and if the guard's torch finds you, you get a star.

WHAT THEY KNOW

  • The police only know what they see or hear: a crime counts when a cop sees it, or hears the gunfire or the blast.
  • They see less far at night, in the rain and in fog.

THE STARS

  • 1 STAR a cop shouts STOP: stand still for 3 s and pay a fine. Keep going and it is 2 stars.
  • 2 STARS they come to arrest you. No shooting unless you fired, rammed a police car or hurt a cop.
  • 3 STARS AND UP cars ram you and cops shoot. An armoured APC from 3 stars - its turret gun fires bursts at you - and at 5 a tank that fires rockets.
  • ROADBLOCKS from 3 stars: police cars across the street ahead, cops behind them shooting - more cars and better guns with more stars, an APC at 5. Ram through or turn off.
  • THE HELICOPTER from 3 stars looks for you with its searchlight: in the light every cop knows where you are (drive faster than it, or get under a car park deck or a tree - on foot, or stopped in a vehicle). Its light alone holds you 6 s: unseen by the cops on the ground that long, it loses you. From 4 stars it fires bursts, at 5 it drops pipe bombs. Only the sniper rifle (3 hits) or the AA launcher brings it down - the next one comes 10 minutes later.

LOSING THEM

  • Get out of sight. While they search, the stars blink and a red circle on the map shows where they are looking.
  • Stay unseen long enough (12 s at 1 star, 50 s at 5) and they give up - it takes longer inside the circle.
  • A HEAT REDUCER takes a star off - even driving through it. From 3 stars one lies on the road ahead of you.
  • A PAY 'N' SPRAY or one of your SAFEHOUSES loses them at any level - if no cop sees you go in. New clothes lose them up to 3 stars.

THE MAP

  • TAB or a tap on the minimap shows the whole city: hospitals (red cross), police stations (blue), the six stores (€), the clothes shops (hangers), the Pay 'n' Spray garages (spray cans), the safehouses (houses: green yours, white with a € for sale), the car park (P), the rampages you have found (skulls), the speedway and the drag strips (chequered flags), a ringing phone and where the police search.
  • The district's name shows as you drive into it.

TRAFFIC

  • Every junction has traffic lights. Cars stop on red, give way when turning across traffic and stop for people crossing; people cross at the zebras on the walk signal.
  • Running a red light yourself is no crime. Ambulances, fire engines and police after you go through red slowly, and traffic keeps right for them.
  • The Colony and the cement works have streets of their own: now and then a car turns in, parks and its driver walks inside; later someone comes out and drives it away.

THE AIRPORT

  • Skyport in the south-west: about once a minute a plane lands from the sea and taxis to one of the five gates, or a parked one is pushed back, taxis out and takes off over the city.
  • Keep off the runway and taxiways: a moving plane runs over anything in its way. Parked planes are solid.
  • Enough gunfire, or a rocket or grenade at its tanks, blows a plane up - a huge blast and a burning wreck.
  • A fence keeps cars off the airfield. At the gates police guard a barrier: stop and it lifts; crash through and you are wanted. Only a heavy vehicle - truck, bus, fire engine, APC, tank - going fast breaks the fence.
  • DEPARTURES at the check-in booth on the terminal's forecourt (a plane on the maps), on foot on its pad: tap FLIGHTS or press E. Prague and Norfolk are coming soon; a SCENIC FLIGHT over the city costs €5,000 - a cinematic at a random hour and weather, two minutes seen from your window seat. LAND (or Esc) skips it.
  • Your first scenic flight gives you an AVIATOR CAP for your wardrobe; every flight after it makes you 50% faster on foot for a day (a wing and the hours left under the clock).
  • The big parking lot is on the avenue, next to the terminal. Bayside Mall stands in a park that covers its whole peninsula, with a pond.

MILITARY BASE

  • North of the airport. Inside the fence a RESTRICTED AREA warning counts down 5 seconds: stay and the alarm gives you 4 STARS and the soldiers open fire.
  • Tanks and APCs in the motor pool, a helicopter on the pad, heavy weapons in the armoury and every kind of ammo in the store inside it. What you take comes back a few minutes after you leave.

PORT, LUNAPARK, SPACE CENTER

  • Dockside is a container port: ships come and go about every two minutes, and the gantry cranes unload them.
  • CAR DELIVERY: the export bay on the south-east corner of the port wants one car at a time - its board says the kind and the colour. Park it in the bay and get out: a crane loads it on the ship and you are paid (less for damage). Not while wanted.
  • Heron Key is a lunapark - coasters, a Ferris wheel, a flume and more. Only on foot, through the turnstiles.
  • Gravel Flats is the space center. Its gate is shut for now.
  • THE COLONY: the big white hotel on the beach east of the start, with its park and fountain; a ring drive round a palm island to the doors, where the valet greets you. CAR PARK (a P on the maps) across from the lunapark gate: stop at the barrier, then drive up the ramps deck by deck to the roof; the decks above you turn see-through, and the police in the street cannot see you up there. Round it a palm grove with a rocky shore and a pier where boats are tied up.
  • 160 UNIQUE BUILDINGS: one-of-a-kind hotels, motels, villas, diners, cinemas, banks, churches, nightclubs, shops and offices, skyscrapers and condo towers, schools, the Sundome arena, parking garages, gas stations, museums, city hall, the library, the courthouse, the post office, supermarkets, car washes and bowling alleys, each with its name in neon on the building (a front seen side-on gets a blade sign toward the camera) - most of the hotels along the beach.
  • 60 PARKS AND PLAZAS: every square, court and park is its own place - fountains, a skate spot, a hedge maze, a carousel, a dog run, a Japanese garden, a lake with swan boats... - its name on a sign by the way in.
  • TOWERS AND WOODS: towers rise behind the street fronts downtown (Palm Heights, Sunstrip, Seaview); the golf links and the big lawns are wooded in patches, the space center's grounds covered in scrub. Below the space center, by the shore, the GRAVEL FLATS CEMENT works smokes from three tall chimneys.
  • TREES are no walls: walk through them; drive into one and it falls - no damage, but it slows you, the lighter the vehicle the more (a motorbike loses 70% of its speed, the tank nothing). They stand again later.

DAY, NIGHT, WEATHER

  • The clock is under the stars. A whole day lasts 24 minutes (New game: CLOCK).
  • A bright, sunny day with sharp shadows; a dark night lit by street lamps, windows, neon and headlights.
  • Rain makes the roads slippery, storms bring lightning, and fog hides you from the police.
  • It is autumn: red, orange and yellow trees, leaves drifting down. At MIDNIGHT there are fireworks over a park, plaza or beach somewhere in the city.

CITY SERVICES

  • Ambulances come for the dead: two paramedics carry the body to the ambulance on a stretcher (two bodies at most per trip) and take it to the nearest hospital with lights and siren. Fire engines put out burning wrecks, and trash trucks stop at the bins.
  • Blasts tear people apart: no body is left, only pieces and blood. Every blood mark has its own shape.

SAVING

  • The game saves itself every minute while no police are after you. CONTINUE picks up the latest save.
  • SLEEP in a safehouse to save in one of three slots (not while you are wanted); there too a save file to download - open it on another device (Load game) to carry on there.
